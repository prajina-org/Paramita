Paramita.woff2 is built from this repository's Paramita.ttf without subsetting.
All glyphs, OpenType shaping data, metrics, hinting, and name records are retained.
Only the DSIG signature is removed during conversion to the WOFF2 container.
The CSS family alias is Paramita; the font's internal family remains Paramita_v1.
